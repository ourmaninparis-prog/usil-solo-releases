## Usil Solo 0.4.0

**New**
- **Ask for access from the app:** the adult enters a name and email, confirms the address via the link received, and the computer links itself once access is accepted. No code to type.
- **Compte card** in the settings: plan, corrections used, trial end, and a link to the online account area (download or delete your data).
- **Automatic updates:** in the background, signed, and installed at a quiet moment.
- **Allowance notes:** short messages for the adult when the allowance is nearly used up or exhausted. The child always gets their text back.

**Install:** download `Usil-Solo-Setup-0.4.0.exe` below and run it. If Windows shows a warning, choose "Informations complémentaires", then "Exécuter quand même".
